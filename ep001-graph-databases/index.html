How graph databases work: Neo4j and Kùzu

Before we build one in C++, why graphs, where the series is heading, and how Neo4j and Kùzu store a graph and run a query.

21 October 2026 · Watch on YouTube

We start a series that builds smollerGraph, a small graph database, from scratch in C++, heading towards a knowledge graph that mvGPT can ask for facts. Before writing any code, we look at the property graph model, why following relationships beats joining tables for queries that walk many hops, and Cypher, on a small graph about animals. Then we run a food chain query on Neo4j and Kùzu, compare how each of them keeps its graph, and write a first graph in C++, kept in memory, with a walk down the food chain and GoogleTest tests.
