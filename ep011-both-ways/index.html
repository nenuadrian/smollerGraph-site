    Reverse traversal and query planning

    Relationships followed both ways in smollerGraph, with labels and types as tokens to make room for a second chain, Cypher that points left, plans that start from a name, and a fair benchmark in Cypher.

    31 October 2026 · Watch on YouTube

    We teach smollerGraph to follow a relationship backwards, from the node it ends at. Its records are full, so labels and relationship types become tokens, as in Neo4j: numbers kept in place of their names, which are kept once each in a store of their own. That makes room for a second chain of relationships for each node, those that end at it. Cypher gets relationships that point left, the planner starts from whichever node is cheapest to find and expands both ways from it, and the page shows both chains. Then we make the benchmark against Neo4j and Kùzu fair: all three answer two questions in Cypher, parsing and planning along with the rest, one following relationships out of a person and one following them in.
