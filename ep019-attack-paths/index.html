    Active Directory attack paths in a graph database

    A made-up company's Active Directory as a graph, and Cypher that follows several relationship types at once, so smollerGraph finds the paths from an account to Domain Admins, the way BloodHound does, in the browser too.

    20 November 2026 · Watch on YouTube

    Attack paths in a company's Active Directory are a shortest path question, and BloodHound made that well known, with Neo4j and Cypher. We make up a company's directory as facts, 275 accounts with their groups and computers, joined by five of the relationships BloodHound draws, MemberOf, AdminTo, HasSession, ForceChangePassword and GenericAll, each pointing the way an attacker would go. smollerGraph's graph, planner and parser learn to follow several relationship types at once, written with bars as Cypher writes them, and 105 of the accounts turn out to have a path to Domain Admins. Taking the backup account out of Domain Admins changes nothing, as it's still an admin on the domain controller, and taking away Engineering's full control of the database's account brings it down to 38. The explorer opens the company in your browser. It's a vanilla take on what BloodHound does, on a company that doesn't exist, built to see how it works.
