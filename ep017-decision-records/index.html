    Architecture decision records for our graph database

    Architecture decision records for smollerGraph, from its first episode's adjacency to last time's docs, each with what forced the choice, what lost to it and what it costs, and how a team proposes, accepts and supersedes them.

    15 November 2026 · Watch on YouTube

    After sixteen episodes, smollerGraph's code says what the database does and each commit what changed, but the reasons behind its choices are in the videos, and the repository keeps none of them. So it gets a folder of architecture decision records, after Michael Nygard's format: one for each choice that would be costly to undo, with the context, the decision, the alternatives considered and the consequences. We go through how a team uses them, proposed in a pull request, accepted as it's merged and superseded instead of rewritten, and then through some of smollerGraph's own, from how it keeps a node's relationships to the record that stopped the races against Neo4j and Kùzu. And the records are published with the API docs.
