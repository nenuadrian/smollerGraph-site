    Property graph storage and WHERE in C++

    Properties for smollerGraph's nodes and relationships, in a chain of records on disk, loaded from facts, asked about with WHERE, and shown in the inspector.

    24 October 2026 · Watch on YouTube

    We give smollerGraph's nodes and relationships properties, kept as in Neo4j's record format: a third file of 32-byte records, each with a key, a value and the next property's id, so a node's properties form a chain, like its relationships. The facts we load get properties in braces, read by our Cypher parser, and Cypher gets WHERE, with each filter planned as early as it can run, and RETURN of any property. The UI's inspector shows a node's properties as they're kept on disk, and we check what they cost a walk.
