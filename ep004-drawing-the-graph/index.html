    Graph visualization in the browser with D3.js

    cpplint, clang-tidy and Abseil's Status for smollerGraph, then a UI, with sg serve answering HTTP and a page that draws the graph with D3, walks it a hop at a time, and shows each node's record on disk.

    22 October 2026 · Watch on YouTube

    We give smollerGraph a UI of its own, after its code gets checked on each push by cpplint, against Google's style guide, and by clang-tidy, and its errors move to Abseil's absl::Status. A new sg serve command answers HTTP requests with the graph as JSON, through cpp-httplib, and a page in the browser draws it with D3's force simulation. A walk lights up the nodes each hop reaches, and clicking a node opens an inspector that shows its record as it's kept on disk, with its page, its offset, its bytes and its chain of relationships.
