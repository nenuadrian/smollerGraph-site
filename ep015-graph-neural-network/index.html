    A graph neural network on a graph database

    A small graph neural network for smollerGraph, a GraphSAGE in PyTorch that learns which species are threatened from their relatives, trained on the graph sg export writes, with its predictions loaded back for Cypher to ask about.

    1 November 2026 · Watch on YouTube

    We train a small graph neural network on smollerGraph's mammals and birds. sg export writes the graph for programs outside the database, and a script trains a GraphSAGE in PyTorch, whose layers each give a node a new vector from its own and the average of its neighbours'. Four layers reach a species' relatives through its genus and its family, and with the label trick, the network learns which species are threatened from what's known about their relatives. It does about as well as a rule written by hand, and its predictions go back into the graph, so Cypher can ask which species listed as least concern it rates at risk. It's a vanilla version of what Neo4j's Graph Data Science library offers, written to see how it works.

    What's missing

    18:57 Our network leaves a lot out, and here's some of what it doesn't do. Its own features are a weight and whether there is one, where a species' diet, its range or its habitat would say more. Its graph has a single kind of relationship, PARENT_TAXON, where a knowledge graph has many, and networks such as relational graph convolutions learn a set of weights for each kind. The SAGE in GraphSAGE stands for sample and aggregate: on a big graph, it averages a sample of each node's neighbours, in batches, where ours averages all of them, all at once. Neo4j's library trains inside the database, and writes back from there too, where ours goes out to a script and back in through sg load. And keep in mind that this is a vanilla version of what the Graph Data Science library offers, built for graphs far bigger than ours, while ours is there to show how it works.
